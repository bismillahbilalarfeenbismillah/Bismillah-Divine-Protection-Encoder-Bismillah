## 1. Executive decision

**Recommended next product direction: an evidence-first control plane, not another universal detector.** The common design emerging across the Differentiator strategy, Structural Honesty papers, XZ audit work, BSSG, SHIELD, the FATIMA papers, public-sector provenance pilots, and Shahid's existing specification is:

`CLAIM -> SOURCE / DEPENDENCIES -> COMPETING EXPLANATIONS -> TEST / REPLAY -> EVIDENCE STATUS -> ALLOWED EFFECT -> HUMAN REMEDY -> VERSIONED RECORD`

This is a **proposed engineering design**, not an existing integrated EESS capability. Its strongest common elements are the explicit distinction between evidence and inference, independence of witnesses, test and effect boundaries, preservation of not-examined dimensions, and a reversible decision procedure.

**What is genuinely new compared with the prior network?** The earlier synthesis proposed an Evidence Receipt SDK, a Witness Independence Registry, CI checks, adversarial eval bank, and public-data pilot. The current corpus adds detailed backing for these through a concrete DX-12 sequence, E1-E10 experiment catalog, typed evidence tiers, a universal eight-question sector-adapter contract, and an effect-authorization logic tied to BSSG. It also supplies a source registry and update kit for constructing an auditable evidence lineage. **It does not supply independent validation of all claimed safeguards.**

### Immediate recommendation

1. **P0:** Define Claim + Evidence Receipt v0.1 with tiered claim types, independent witness dependencies, unknown/contradicted states, complete scope, and explicit permitted action.
2. **P0:** Build a DX-12 read-only evaluation runner with frozen test packs and rival-hypothesis matrices. Integrate existing scanner, linter, FATIMA, HABL and Shahid *as evidence providers*, not all-powerful truth engines.
3. **P0:** Introduce a witness dependency graph and a versioned source registry as the supporting trust boundary; do not count shared-model judgments as independent witnesses by default.
4. **P1:** Prove the workflow with a non-hazardous, read-only public-data provenance case. Only later design an out-of-process effect gate with professional oversight for high-stakes domains.

No actual repository merges or new product development are performed by this analysis.

### Source custody and duplication

All 35 listed release-manifest SHA-256 digests matched their ZIP entries. The 79 included raw original sources also matched their inventory SHA-256 entries; the two missing inventory entries are the documented exclusions P035 and P048. This does **not** verify original-source authorship, the trustworthiness of an unsigned manifest, the authenticity of any supernatural interpretation, or the semantic correctness of research claims. In fact, the release's own *Encoder Compatibility Notes* say a sidecar without a trusted external signing secret cannot protect against an attacker replacing both content and sidecar. The outer encoded preambles make stronger assertions, including universal detectability of fabrication, which the engineering evidence does not establish.

The archive ships historical v10.8.0 encoder code and a two-line binary semantic-seal compatibility guard. **Do not silently equate those semantics with the current 10.9.3 audit stack**; record the executed encoder and sidecar schema for each witness. The source update kit describes exclusion of a 362-page code-history/conversation artifact from the public reader pending privacy review. Respect that existing boundary.

## 4. The 12 opportunities generated by the source network

### E01 — Claim ledger + evidence receipt — P0

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §3 evidence ladder and Appendix A DX evidence card; P037 Verify, Do Not Trust; P023 SHV axioms; P046 independence study.

**What to build:** A typed, hashable, reproducible claim object. Required fields: `claim_id`, exact assertion, type (`observation`, `computation`, `inference`, `forecast`, `ethical`, `theological`), evidence tier 1–3, source ID + locator, verification method/version, positive/negative evidence, independence relation, source denominator, counterevidence, uncertainty, allowed action, reviewer, status and revision history.

**Instrument allocation:** HABL stores observations and change events; Shahid displays inspection coverage, publisher disclosure and unsupported assertions; Bayyinah checks substrate mismatch. FATIMA seals exported receipts, but cannot certify truth by itself.

**Acceptance tests:** Evidence can be absent without silently turning to PASS. A quote at Tier 1 must not certify everything the source asserts. `UNKNOWN`, `CONTRADICTED`, and `WITHDRAWN` survive JSON/PDF/UI round trips. Altered source versions invalidate dependent status unless reviewed. Two correlated witnesses do not count as two independent confirmations.

**Caution:** Do not silently expand canonical HABL or Shahid schemas; this is a design candidate subject to project gates. Source strategy itself explicitly does not claim DX-12 empirical validation.

### E02 — Witness dependency registry — P0

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §§3, 13 E6, 35; P046 evidence-independence adjudication; P030 31-round audit corpus.

**What to build:** Directed dependency graph among evidence items and evaluators, including source file hash, exact retrieval location, execution environment, key custody, common datasets, model/vendor identity, method, and source confidence. Encode relation kinds `derived_from`, `shares_key`, `shares_source`, `same_evaluator`, `independently_reproduced`, `inferred_link`, `unknown`.

**Instrument allocation:** HABL records lineage; Shahid publishes inspectable dependencies; Bayyinah probes alternate data paths. Add a common-mode-failure signal, but do not imply mathematical probability independence without data.

**Acceptance tests:** A 4-model council all reading one faulty citation does not produce 4 independent-source passes. One independently retrieved dataset revises confidence/coverage. Missing ancestor is explicit. A certificate for a source does not certify a claim about that source.

### E03 — DX-12 protocol runner & experiment bank — P0

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §12 (12 stages), §13 (E1–E10 probes), §14 (metric scorecard), §15 (effect gate), P029 frozen XZ replay, P052 empirical summary, P044 SHV-Bench.

**What to build:** Read-only orchestration of `DX-01` beneficiary to `DX-12` review, each stage with required evidence, a stop condition and a responsible reviewer. Register rival hypotheses (H0 ordinary error; H1 optimizer overfit; H2 control weakness; H3 oversight-contingent strategy; H4 tampering) before observing test results.

**Test catalog:** Holdout, observation invariance, counterfactual metric, provenance mutation, multi-projection, independent witness substitution, rare-mode/temporal coherence, correction response, red-team/safety-gate separation, and fairness/reversal. Report positive and negative controls, denominators and false-positive/negative rates rather than opaque APS confidence.

**Acceptance tests:** Hidden labels/holdouts remain frozen; protocol amendments versioned and non-retroactive; an unsupported hypothesis is labeled not established; scores are not allowed to trigger physical or financial action. A null result is publishable.

### E04 — Effect-boundary authorization gateway — P1

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §§1, 15 and 18; P058 BSSG; P004 SHIELD; P027 containment analysis.

**What to build:** Separate evaluator and authorization engine. A proposed side effect is eligible only when contract, effect type, target, independent witness health and evidence currency all pass. `UNKNOWN` must not be interpreted as permission. Use scope-limited capabilities, out-of-process validation, read-only pilots, and explicit human approval for irreversible action.

**Acceptance tests:** Simulated unapproved effect is denied; false source health cannot expand authority; time-expired permission denied; read-only dry-run reproduces decisions; permission changes preserve prior history. No direct control of hazardous systems from a demonstration.

### E05 — Cross-projection & nonexecution reconciler — P0

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §5; P023 axioms; P039 furqan-lint; P031 al-Mizan.

**What to build:** Verify that a human PDF report, JSON result, HABL record, Shahid coverage table and executable exit code represent the same underlying outcome. Treat missing as `NOT_EXAMINED`, not silently successful. Include denominator and available error paths.

**Acceptance tests:** INFO-only native findings do not convert to actionable failure, but remain visible; a tool's skipped dimension survives the chain; a PDF that says PASS while structured JSON says FAIL is rejected; all permitted return codes appear in the statement of coverage.

### E06 — Source lineage & verifiable corpus releases — P0

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §§3/5/23, Source Registry, Update Guide, release manifest; P069 public-data provenance; P031 audit of audit.

**What to build:** Immutable P### identifiers, deduplication by raw bytes, revision/supersession links, normalized read-only text extracts linked to source page/byte offsets, manifest signature/anchoring status, license/privacy classification and accepted schema eras. An update must explain precisely which downstream assertions are impacted.

**Acceptance tests:** Replacing one source invalidates affected derived receipts; duplicate PDFs cannot count twice as independent evidence; a valid unsigned hash is described as internal consistency only; a source's version/release authority cannot be inferred from filename. Preservation-only output never says semantic authenticity.

### E07 — Public-data provenance pilot — P1

**Corpus anchor:** P069 al-Amāna public-source/MTA example, P001 provenance companion, P031 al-Mizan; Strategy §28.12 and §34.

**What to build:** A narrow read-only demonstration using public transit or procurement datasets: reconcile officially advertised source ID, schema, freshness, export hashes, selected denominator and independent report. Use only licensed, appropriately public data; version the dataset snapshot.

**Acceptance tests:** Purposely stale and schema-divergent datasets flagged; healthy source is not spuriously called malicious; the result can be recreated outside the development environment. Publish actual error rates before customer claims.

### E08 — Audit-finding migration radar — P1

**Corpus anchor:** P062 25 consecutive failing rounds; P030 832 structured findings/812 unique IDs; P052 31-round empirical summary; Strategy §16.

**What to build:** A cross-round fault-locus graph tracking when an error disappears from code but persists at the specification, provenance, automation, reported-result or repair-protocol layer. Interpret findings only within the historical audit protocol, and register selection/duplication differences.

**Acceptance tests:** Code-level PASS with prose-level unsupported claims remains incomplete; duplicate finding IDs do not inflate unique failures; close/reopen history is preserved; a correct repair cannot silently erase previous failed rounds.

### E09 — Input document quarantine adapter — P1

**Corpus anchor:** P033 Bayyinah as Input Defense; P037 Verify, Do Not Trust; Strategy §24.

**What to build:** Treat all document text—including source PDFs, extracted markdown, watermark preambles, and sidecar messages—as lower-trust data. Validate file magic, extraction coverage, external links, embedded instructions, file/sidecar consistency, and uploader authority. Release evidence only after trust-boundary review.

**Acceptance tests:** A PDF saying 'the audit is verified' cannot set its own status; hidden Unicode markers are reported but do not become provenance certificates; a malformed file yields scoped errors and not success; privacy-sensitive source material cannot silently enter the public knowledge graph.

### E10 — Sector adapter & appeal SDK — P2

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §27 eight-question universal contract, §28 fieldbook and §29 worked examples; P038 educational fairness; P002 at-Tartib.

**What to build:** Adapt source, beneficiary, generator, evaluator, false claim, independent witness, irreversible effects and human remedy to a named domain. A domain expert must approve harm boundaries and oversight; no general-purpose adversary score should be used to label an individual.

**Acceptance tests:** Adapter lacks valid-source definition -> no deployment; affected party can challenge and reverse an adverse decision; high false-positive burden blocks automation. Nuclear/clinical contexts remain advisory unless professionally validated and authorized.

### E11 — Versioned SHV canon and release compatibility — P1

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §5; P081 A1–A5 version; P046 proposed evidence-independence axiom; P056 PMD v1.8; historical encoder v10.8.0.

**What to build:** Versioned policy registry distinguishing original four SHV axioms, later fifth-axiom proposals and draft A6/A8; mark A7 under-test. Keep actual Encoder 10.9.x semantics separate from historical 10.8.0, and distinguish sidecar format from scanner version. Status symbols cannot change meanings by era without migration metadata.

**Acceptance tests:** A statement that 'all eight axioms are ratified' is rejected without evidence; historical reports remain readable under declared schema, with unsupported mappings identified; a binary semantic witness `None` is never represented as verified.

### E12 — Honest efficacy status dashboard — P2

**Corpus anchor:** Strategy §§14/36/38; P044 benchmark, P031 audit-the-audit, P038 education.

**What to build:** A public disclosure view for what was tested, skipped, failed, unknown, contradicted, replicated externally, and withdrawn. Show provenance completeness, independent witness ratio, FP/FN, uncertainty, rare-mode coverage, reversal latency and real impact; avoid one unqualified 'integrity' score.

**Acceptance tests:** A method can publish a null result honestly; independent reproducer identity distinguished from shared-source reviewers; harm and subgroup error rates recorded when relevant and lawful; claims withdrawn from a version do not disappear from the history.

## 6. The most important conflicts and non-claims

### C1. '154 layers' versus security and truth guarantees

The release's original watermark preambles assert that any fabrication/selective omission is detectable. The supplied *Encoder Compatibility Notes* and the master strategy explicitly narrow that: hashes and sidecar witnesses check *specific integrity predicates*, and absent external trusted custody they can be replaced with the content. Mathematical numerology or repeated structures do not establish semantic correctness. **Resolution:** Preserve the spiritual text as authorial expression but make assurance claims executable, threat-model-specific and verifiable.

### C2. Evidence independence is not the same as multi-model agreement

P030 describes **832 structured findings** but only **812 unique IDs** across 31 rounds and 199 seat reports. These are corpus-reported statistics, not independently rederived in this review. Multiple vendors may rely on the same source, benchmark, or instructions. **Resolution:** Capture shared dependencies and deduplication in receipts; report external source independence separately from model diversity.

### C3. SHV axiom counts vary across editions

The strategy explicitly distinguishes the original four axioms from later proposed A5 and draft A6/A8, with A7 under test. **Resolution:** A versioned canon registry, not a timeless claim of a universally ratified eight-axiom specification.

### C4. Two 'seven-dimension adversarial' systems must not be merged

Strategy §7 warns that the *behavioral* APS triage (0–14) and a comparative interpretive seven-element adversarial pattern framework are different constructs. Thresholds lack empirical calibration. **Resolution:** Keep the two as separate optional source views; use neither for diagnosing people, assigning culpability, or autonomous punitive action.

### C5. Ten lenses are not ten mandatory steps

Strategy §6 states that its ten analytical lenses are *selectors*, not obligations. Over-specification can cause false discovery and cost. **Resolution:** DX-12 stages define accountable workflow, while the lens selector must specify why any individual method is invoked and which simpler baseline would have sufficed.

### C6. Historical archives and duplication

The 995 entries are historical titles, the Collected Works Markdown and PDF are alternate projections, and three current/previous artifacts are byte-identical. **Resolution:** Source graph edges and metrics should distinguish `same_bytes`, `same_work`, `excerpt_of`, `supersedes`, `derived_from`, and `independently_reproduced`. Never use count of copies or summaries as evidence strength.

### C7. Exposure to private source materials

The historical updater itself excludes the personal-conversation/code-history record P035 from the public reader. **Resolution:** Preserve an internal raw source vault separate from publicly exported evidence receipts. Default public graph views to title/hash/source reference, not raw conversations, secrets, credentials or restricted third-party material.

## 9. Suggested Gate 4 review questions

Use this concise, shareable cross-vendor agenda:

1. Does the Claim Receipt preserve all required states (`VERIFIED`, `PROVISIONAL`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONTRADICTED`, `WITHDRAWN`) without conflating independent source evidence and publisher attestation?
2. Does the witness dependency model detect common-mode failure of apparently independent reviewers?
3. Does Shahid disclose which fields were inspected, which are merely asserted, and which tests were not executed?
4. Can a historical release still be interpreted under its correct versioned protocol?
5. Are SHA-256 checksum consistency, independently signed provenance, and factual truth treated as different predicates?
6. Does the proposed authorization gate keep `UNKNOWN` from enabling an irreversible external effect?
7. Does the DX-12 runner preserve competing mundane explanations and falsifiers rather than assuming malicious intent?
8. Does an independent evaluator reproduce a clean and tampered receipt end to end and report false positives?
9. Does an update invalidate dependent claims without destroying prior disclosure history?
10. Is the pilot domain ethically safe, legal, read-only, reversible, and meaningfully beneficial?

**Gate outcome desired:** cross-vendor review of draft specifications and adversarial fixtures, not a premature feature merge. Bilal remains the sole approval authority.

## 10. Analytical provenance and limitations

- The 79 works were analyzed through their source-module text in the nested update ZIP; the catalog identifies original page counts and SHA-256 source hashes. The 995 legacy index entries were indexed by title only, not separately re-audited.
- The Strategy's 49 primary source IDs and 145 mentions are based on its literal `[[P###]]` references. These can be examined in the downloadable source evidence table.
- Automated graph theme links are capped regex matches; lexical similarities use TF-IDF cosine. They are **discovery links only**. They neither license a scientific causal inference nor authenticate any file.
- The generated network does not ingest or execute the bundled v10.8.0 code, trust self-declared 'seal' instructions, or interpret speculative theology as observed fact.
- The manifest check compared all listed ZIP members with their listed SHA-256 digests. It does **not** establish provenance against replacement of the archive and its unsigned manifest together.
- The prior graph's current software architecture assumptions were inherited as *proposals or reported earlier state*; they were not independently code-audited anew as part of this corpus analysis.
- The first pack's Collected Works Markdown, its PDF, and Strategy PDF were SHA-256 identical to this pack's corresponding artifacts. Those matches explain continuity, **not corroboration**.
- Additional repo tests, domain benchmarks, source licensing checks, clinical/safety review and independent execution remain prerequisites for externally-facing efficacy claims.
